3. Cookie policy

A cookie is a small file that a website stores in your browser. This website does not use its own cookies or third-party cookies for analytics or advertising, so it does not need to display a cookie banner.

  • Fonts and images are served from our own server, without connecting to external services.
  • Google map: the map in the contact section only loads if you click “Load map”. At that point, Google may set cookies and process browsing data under its privacy policy. If you do not click the button, no connection to Google is made.
  • External links (WhatsApp, AEMET, Spanish Maritime Rescue, Google Maps): when you follow them, you leave this website and those services' cookie policies apply.

If we add cookies that are not strictly necessary in the future, such as analytics cookies, we will say so here and ask for your consent before setting them. You can delete cookies at any time in your browser settings.
