Decisions backed by reliable technical information

Before buying a yacht, when renewing its documentation or when it needs to be valued for an insurer, you need a rigorous and independent technical report. Our technical office coordinates inspections, valuations and certifications on the Costa del Sol and handles the related paperwork with the authorities.

Our technical services

  • Pre-purchase condition surveys: hull, structure, engines, systems and equipment.
  • Technical Inspections of Vessels (ITB): periodic, intermediate and extraordinary.
  • CE marking and certification of pleasure craft.
  • Valuations and expert appraisals for insurance, finance, inheritance or sale.
  • Tonnage Certificate.

Pre-purchase survey: before you sign

A condition survey before buying reveals hidden defects, helps estimate repair costs and gives you solid grounds to negotiate the price. Insurers and lenders often require one too. We can combine it with tax advice and the change of ownership, so you have a single point of contact for the whole transaction.

CE marking for imported yachts

Pleasure craft placed on the market or put into service in the EU must comply with the European Recreational Craft Directive and carry the CE mark. If you are importing a yacht built outside the EU or without CE marking, we guide you through assessment and certification.

Frequently asked questions

When should I commission a survey?

Always before buying a second-hand yacht, especially if it is of a certain length or age. Also before taking out insurance or applying for finance.

What is the ITB?

The ITB is Spain's mandatory periodic technical inspection, which checks that the yacht still meets safety and seaworthiness requirements. We coordinate the inspection and the renewal of certificates.

Where do you carry out surveys?

Mainly in the ports and marinas of the Costa del Sol. For other locations, please ask.
