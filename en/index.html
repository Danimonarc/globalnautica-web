About us

A complete service, tailored to every yacht

Global Nautica was founded on the Costa del Sol in 1999 to provide a complete service to the nautical sector, specialising in pleasure yachts.

Our experience, combined with constant adaptation to regulatory, tax, administrative and technological changes, allows us to find real solutions for our clients.

Our approach is simple: every client and every yacht is different. That is why we offer tailored proposals from a wide range of options.

We are proud of our high client loyalty and many returning clients, because we propose solutions that fit each client's specific needs at every moment.

Services

Three areas, one point of contact

We cover the three branches of pleasure boating. Our team will be happy to give you a personal service.

Yacht tax & customs

Our in-depth knowledge of the EU Customs Code allows us to present every available option within a tailored project, taking into account the client's needs and the characteristics of the yacht.

  • Specialist tax advice
  • Permanent import into the EU
  • Temporary admission in the EU
  • Release for free circulation
  • Exports
  • Inward processing relief
  • Customs formalities

Always focused on the most favourable solution for the client.

More about yacht tax & VAT →

Administration & registration

We take care of all your yacht's paperwork, in Spain and abroad, so you can focus on sailing.

  • Yacht registration in Spain, EU countries and non-EU flags
  • Commercial structures (charter, List 6)
  • Advice on buying and selling yachts
  • Change of owner, name, home port, list, etc.
  • Certificate of Seaworthiness and Navigation Permit
  • Matriculation tax (IEDMT) and transfer tax (ITP)
  • Technical Inspection of Vessels (ITB)
  • Ship Station Licence (LEB)
  • MMSI number
  • Charter authorisations in Spain for foreign-flagged yachts
  • Commercial and tourist activity licences in Andalusian ports
  • Crew management: sign-on / sign-off and minimum crew
  • High-speed craft operator registration (Spanish Tax Agency)
  • Fishing licences for the yacht
  • Renewal of boating licences
  • Bringing your yacht's paperwork up to date
More about yacht registration →

Technical office

  • Condition reports for buying and selling (surveyor service)
  • Periodic, intermediate and extraordinary technical inspections (ITB)

  • Valuations and expert appraisals
  • Tonnage Certificate
